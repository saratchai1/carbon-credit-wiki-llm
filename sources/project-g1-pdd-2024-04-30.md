# Source Note — Group 1 PDD

## Scope
Registered-project design evidence for the Premium T-VER mangrove reforestation project Group 1. Validation Report identifies the reviewed PDD as revision 06 dated 2024-04-30.

## Claims extracted
- Project: โครงการปลูกป่าชายเลนช่วยโลกลดก๊าซเรือนกระจกในประเทศไทย (กลุ่ม 1) / Greenhouse Gas Reduction with Mangrove Reforestation in Thailand (Group 1).
- Owner/developer: Department of Marine and Coastal Resources (DMCR); co-developers Siam TC Technology Co., Ltd. and Wisut Consultant Co., Ltd.
- 40 project plots across 7 provinces.
- Allocated area: 1,472.13 rai; final project area after exclusions: 1,195.64 rai.
- Province totals: Trat 8 plots/108.98 rai; Rayong 4/121.94; Nakhon Si Thammarat 2/500.76; Phang Nga 15/168.46; Satun 8/252.25; Pattani 2/14.71; Krabi 1/28.54.
- Site-condition strata: former shrimp ponds 12 plots/230.92 rai; cleared/planting-ready areas 24 plots/449.25 rai; accreting mudflat areas 4 plots/515.47 rai.
- Crediting period: 2023-10-01 to 2038-09-30 (15 years).
- Ex-ante project estimate: 169,725.08 tCO2e over the crediting period, reported as approximately 11,315 tCO2e/year.
- Baseline tree carbon change is treated as zero subject to conditions that pre-project trees are not removed/destroyed and are excluded from project-credit tree accounting.
- Project emissions from biomass burning are not considered because no burning is planned; fossil-fuel emissions are not considered under the small-project treatment stated in the PDD.
- Leakage is reported as zero/not applicable in the ex-ante calculation.
- Credit-sharing framework in the PDD: Siam TC up to 85%, Wisut Consultant up to 5%, DMCR at least 10%, or as otherwise agreed; part of the DMCR share is described for local coastal-community support.

## Methodology / tools
- T-VER-P-METH-13-02 v01 — Afforestation/Reforestation of Degraded Mangrove Habitats.
- T-VER-P-TOOL-01-01 v01 — baseline/additionality for forestry project activities.
- T-VER-P-TOOL-01-02 v01 — tree carbon stocks and changes.
- T-VER-P-TOOL-01-05 v01 — non-CO2 emissions from biomass burning.

## Monitoring and carbon pools
- Monitoring approach: stratified random sampling; satellite imagery supports canopy-cover assessment and stratification.
- Tree carbon includes aboveground and belowground biomass.
- Optional sapling and dead-wood pools are not included in this registered calculation.
- SOC is included in this project calculation.
- Project tree-growth monitoring is specified at least every 3 years for the carbon-MRV plan.
- For planted trees <4.5 cm diameter and >1.3 m height, the PDD describes D0/root-collar diameter measurement; when diameter >=4.5 cm and height >1.3 m, DBH at 1.3 m is used.
- PDD describes Komiyama et al. (2005) allometry for aboveground/belowground biomass in the sampling approach.
- If calculated uncertainty exceeds 10%, conservative deduction is required according to the applicable tool/table.

## Ex-ante parameters
- Project area used for carbon calculation: 1,195.64 rai.
- Tree MAI used for initial ex-ante estimate: 9.40 tCO2e/rai/year for Rhizophora spp.
- Initial tree component: 11,239.02 tCO2e/year; 168,585.24 tCO2e over 15 years.
- Default SOC parameter cited: dSOC = 0.26 tC/rai/year, with methodology text stating applicability from planting year through planting year +20 unless another value is demonstrated.

## Applicability
Use as Tier-2 registered-project design evidence. It supports project configuration and ex-ante assumptions, not actual verified monitoring-period removals or issued credits.

## Supersedes / conflicts
- Final project area 1,195.64 rai supersedes use of the original allocated area 1,472.13 rai for project carbon accounting.
- SOC implementation in the accompanying workbook requires reconciliation with the methodology wording; see `PROJECT-G1-CALC-2024-04-30`.
