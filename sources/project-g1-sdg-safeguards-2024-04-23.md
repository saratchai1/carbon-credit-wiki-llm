# Source Note — Group 1 SD & Safeguards

## Scope
Project evidence for Sustainable Development Goals and safeguards monitoring under Premium T-VER.

## Claims extracted
- Project covers 40 plots in 7 provinces.
- SDG 10 (Reduced Inequality): indicator is employment/income generation; monitoring frequency 1 time/year using interviews and contract documents.
- SDG 13 (Climate Action): indicator is seedling/tree growth; monitoring described every 5 years using sample plots to assess growth and increased carbon sequestration.
- SDG 14 (Life Below Water): indicators include survival and growth / mangrove restoration; monitoring frequency 1 time/year using satellite imagery to assess project-area change.
- Satellite imagery is therefore part of the documented project monitoring framework, not merely an optional visualization layer.

## Monitoring-cadence interpretation
Do not collapse all monitoring into one interval. The project package contains multiple cadences for different purposes:
- annual SD/community/ecological indicators,
- annual satellite-based area/change monitoring for SDG 14,
- 5-year SDG 13 growth sampling in the SD/Safeguards report,
- at least 3-year tree-carbon MRV monitoring in the PDD.

These cadences serve different requirements and should be stored separately in digital MRV systems.

## Applicability
Use for SD/Safeguards monitoring design and evidence requirements. Do not use SDG-monitoring frequency as a substitute for the PDD carbon-MRV frequency.
