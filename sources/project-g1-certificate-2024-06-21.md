- Certificate date: 2024-06-21.
- Expected greenhouse-gas reduction/removal: 11,315 tCO2e/year.
- Crediting period: 2023-10-01 through 2038-09-30.
- Project parties named include DMCR, Siam TC Technology Co., Ltd., and Wisut Consultant Co., Ltd.

## Critical status interpretation
This certificate supports `REGISTERED_PROJECT` status and the registered ex-ante expectation. It is not evidence that 11,315 credits are issued every year, and it is not a Monitoring/Verification issuance record.

Do not label the 11,315 tCO2e/year value as `CERTIFIED_CREDIT` unless separate TGO issuance/certification evidence for an actual monitoring period is ingested.
