# Source Note — Group 1 Validation Report

## Scope
Independent validation evidence for the Group 1 Premium T-VER project and the PDD revision dated 2024-04-30.

## Claims extracted
- VVB: VGreen KU Co., Ltd.
- Validation opinion: `CERTIFY`.
- Project: 40 plots across 7 provinces.
- Methodology confirmed: T-VER-P-METH-13-02 v01.
- Expected removal/sequestration: 11,315 tCO2e/year over a 15-year crediting period, 2023-10-01 to 2038-09-30.
- Baseline reported as 0.00 tCO2e and leakage as 0.00 tCO2e in the validated project package.
- Validation was performed at a reasonable level of assurance as stated in the report.

## Validation findings
The validator recorded:
- 2 Material Misstatements,
- 1 Misstatement below materiality,
- 19 Nonconformities.

The report states that the project provided clarifications/supporting evidence and updated the PDD revision 06 dated 2024-04-30, after which the issues were closed.

### Material boundary findings of long-term importance
- `MM 01`: project coordinates were not identified in an earlier PDD revision.
- `MM 02`: project boundary was unclear and areas not participating in the project had not been excluded.

These findings support the rule that the final 1,195.64-rai project boundary, not the original 1,472.13-rai allocation, is the accounting boundary to use.

## Applicability
Tier-2 validation evidence. Confirms design/methodology/package validity at registration stage. It is not verification of actual monitoring-period removals and not evidence of credit issuance.

## Supersedes / conflicts
Earlier project-boundary descriptions that retain all allocated land are superseded for carbon accounting by the corrected validated PDD boundary.
