# Monitoring / MRV — STC + VSD Standard T-VER Group 2

## Satellite / stratification

The registered PDD uses Landsat-8 Forest Canopy Density (FCD) information to stratify baseline conditions and allocate sample plots.

Dimensions:
- Gulf of Thailand vs Andaman coast
- high FCD `>65%`
- medium FCD `30–65%`
- low FCD `<30%`

The PDD describes vegetation, bare-soil and shadow-index inputs in the FCD workflow.

## Field plots

- 40 × 40 m = 1 rai
- 20 × 40 m = 0.5 rai

Tree records include count, species, tag, GBH and DBH.

## Rhizophora measurement point

Where prop roots rise above the normal 1.30 m DBH level, measure diameter **30 cm above the prop-root point**.

## Monitoring frequencies observed in PDD

| Parameter/evidence | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Project coordinates | every 5 years |
| Tree carbon stock `CTT_t` | every 5 years |
| Tree diameter/DBH survey | every 3 years |
| Burned area | every 5 years |
| Fossil-fuel-use records | at least monthly |

Burned-area evidence may use field survey and satellite/aerial imagery.

## Architecture rule

This registered project supports:

`remote sensing → stratification / area / disturbance evidence`

`field measurement → tree biomass/carbon primary evidence`

Do not use direct `tree count / NDVI / FCD → tCO2eq credit` conversion without a methodology-valid calibrated model, uncertainty treatment and approval/evidence chain.
