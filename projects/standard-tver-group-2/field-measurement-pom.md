# Group 2 — Field Measurement and POM

## Baseline field measurement

The registered PDD describes measurement of:
- tree number / tree ID
- species
- DBH
- tagged trees in sample plots
- calibrated measurement equipment before field measurement

## Normal DBH point

Normal DBH is measured at **1.3 m above ground**.

For use in the project equations, the PDD states the tree should have:
- DBH >= **4.5 cm**
- height > **1.3 m**

## Rhizophora / prop-root POM

For large- or small-leaved Rhizophora where prop roots extend above 1.3 m, the PDD describes measuring **30 cm above the highest prop root that is securely grounded**, or at another appropriate point consistent with the applicable forestry guidance/allometric conditions.

This project-specific rule is directly relevant to field DBH and LiDAR-derived DBH QA/QC. Store a `measurement_point_method`/POM flag per tree when deriving measurements digitally.
