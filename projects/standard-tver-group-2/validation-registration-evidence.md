## Conflict / ambiguity register

### C-001 — annual vs total quantity

A PDD sentence can be read as if `84,652 tCO2eq` were the total amount over 10 years. This conflicts with the calculation workbook, Validation Report and registration certificate.

Resolution:
- **84,652.17 tCO2eq/year** = validated annual average
- **84,652 tCO2eq/year** = certificate rounded value
- **846,521.70 tCO2eq** = net 10-year total

Status: `RESOLVED_BY_HIGHER_CONFIDENCE_EVIDENCE`.

### C-002 — PDD date/version identity

The Validation Report identifies the PDD it validated as version 2 dated **23 Jun 2024**. The Drive folder also contains files named `MOC2 standard_V2(22-8-24)`.

The Wiki must not assume the Aug-named copy is byte-identical to the PDD validated in June without direct document comparison/hash evidence.

Status: `VERSION_IDENTITY_UNCONFIRMED`.

## Credit-status caution

Registration/validation of an ex-ante PDD is not the same as verification of monitored removals and is not issuance evidence. Do not classify these expected quantities as `CERTIFIED_CREDIT`.
