# Group 2 — Monitoring

## Registered monitoring design extracted from the PDD

### Project boundary

The PDD specifies project-location/boundary monitoring every **5 years** using GPS, undertaken by DMCR together with Siam TC Technology.

### Area risk monitoring

The PDD identifies collection and assessment of project-area risks including:
- disease and insects
- encroachment

These are to be considered around the monitoring/evaluation cycle for certification.

## Implications for the current MRV system

The modern satellite/drone/LiDAR monitoring stack may support QA/QC, stratification, change detection and field targeting, but it must remain traceable to the registered project boundary and field measurement rules.

Where a monitoring calculation relies on the registered assumptions of zero project emissions or zero leakage, reconfirm the activity evidence for the relevant monitoring period rather than assuming the ex-ante condition remains true.
