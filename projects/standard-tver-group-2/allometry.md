# Group 2 — Allometry

## Registered PDD equations

The PDD uses common mangrove allometric equations attributed to Komiyama et al. (2005).

Aboveground biomass:

`ABG = 0.251 × rho × D^2.46`

Belowground biomass:

`BLG = 0.199 × rho^0.899 × D^2.22`

Where:
- `D` = tree diameter in cm at the applicable measurement point
- `rho` = average wood density in g dry weight/cm3
- `ABG`, `BLG` = dry biomass quantities according to the equation definitions

Carbon-to-CO2 conversion in the PDD/tool chain uses:

`44/12`

## Important limits

- Wood density/species mapping must come from the approved project source or applicable tool; do not invent `rho`.
- The exact carbon fraction used by the registered workbook should be read from the project calculation/PDD before reproducing tree-level values; this page intentionally does not infer an untranscribed value.
- The registered project excludes SOC, deadwood and litter from the accounting calculation.

## Reference

Komiyama A, Poungparn S, Kato S. Common allometric equations for estimating the tree weight of mangroves. Journal of Tropical Ecology. 2005;21(4):471–477.
