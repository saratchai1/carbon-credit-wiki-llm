# Group 2 — Baseline

## Registered baseline value

The calculation workbook records baseline tree sequestration/stock of:

**546,011.7485172224 tCO2eq**

Rounded for normal reporting: **546,011.75 tCO2eq**.

## Basis

Baseline field surveys recorded at least:
- tree count
- species (common/scientific/family information in the PDD workflow)
- tagged tree ID
- DBH / circumference-derived stem diameter

The field data were used to estimate tree density and biomass within stratified sample plots.

## Carbon pools

The registered design includes tree aboveground and belowground biomass. It explicitly excludes:
- soil organic carbon
- deadwood
- litter

The exclusion was justified in the PDD by the large project area and difficulty of repeat measurement/monitoring.

## Accounting caution

This baseline belongs to the registered Standard T-VER Group 2 design. Do not transfer it into a Premium T-VER calculation or another project boundary.
